Your passwords and team secrets, locked before they leave your Mac.

Zvault is a password manager in the spirit of 1Password. Everything is encrypted on your device with keys only you hold. Share a password with a link, give your team the right environments, and let AI agents use secrets only when you approve.

How it works

Zvault can't read your data. Not even if it wanted to.

Your master password and a random Secret Key are stretched into your account key on your Mac. Items are sealed before they are synced, so the server only ever holds ciphertext.

  1. 🔑

    Master password + Secret Key

    The Secret Key is made on your Mac and printed in your Emergency Kit PDF.

  3. ⚙️

    Argon2id

    A memory-hard key derivation that makes guessing slow and expensive.

  5. 🔒

    XChaCha20-Poly1305

    Every item is encrypted and authenticated on your device in a Rust core.

  7. ☁️

    Server sees ciphertext

    Sign-in uses SRP, so your password is never sent, not even in hashed form.

Features

Everything a password manager should do, and a few things for developers.

🛡️

Zero-knowledge encryption

Argon2id, a 128-bit Secret Key and XChaCha20-Poly1305. Keys never leave your Mac.

📄

Emergency Kit

A PDF with your Secret Key to print or store safely, needed to sign in on a new device.

🔢

Two-step sign-in

Set up TOTP by scanning a QR code, with recovery codes. Store one-time codes for other sites too.

🗝️

Vault

Logins, notes and websites with search, copy and reveal, synced across your devices. See every signed-in device and sign any of them out.

✨

Password generator

Random passwords or passphrases, plus a strength check that estimates cracking time.

👆

Auto-lock and Touch ID

Locks when idle, on sleep or screen lock. Unlock with Touch ID. Copied secrets clear themselves.

🔗

Secure share links

Share an item with a link that expires and allows a set number of views. It's decrypted in the recipient's browser.

👥

Share with people

Send items to other Zvault users, encrypted to their key, and by email.

🗂️

Projects and environments

Group team secrets by project, environment and folder. Each environment has its own key.

🎛️

Teams and access roles

Manage, Edit, Use or Needs approval, per environment. Production stays with the people who need it.

⌨️

The zv CLI

Read secrets, load an environment, or run a command with secrets injected and masked.

🤖

AI agent access

Pair tools like Claude Code. They ask, you approve in the app, and every use is logged.

For developers

Secrets in your terminal, never in your .env files.

zv talks to the Zvault app on your Mac. The app asks you to approve, then hands over only what the command needs. Output from zv run is masked.

  • zv read one secret, or zv copy it to the clipboard
  • zv env prints an environment as dotenv or shell variables
  • zv run starts a command with secrets in its environment
  • zv agent pair connects an AI agent that you approve in the app

Install Zvault in a few minutes.

        

        

          

            
The Mac app

            
    
              
  1. 
                Download the Zvault .dmg from the
                latest release.
              
    2. 
              
  2. Open it and drag Zvault into Applications.
    3.

    4. 
              
  4. Create an account with your email. We send you a code to confirm it.
    5. 
              
  5. Choose a master password and save your Emergency Kit PDF somewhere safe.

            Download for macOS
          

          

            
The zv command line

            
Easiest: in the app, open Settings → Command line → Install CLI.

            
Or install it yourself:

            

              
mkdir -p ~/.local/bin && curl -fsSL https://github.com/bhagyesh-r-zymr/Zvault/releases/latest/download/zv-macos-universal -o ~/.local/bin/zv && chmod +x ~/.local/bin/zv

              
            

            
Then check it can reach the app:

            

              
zv status
